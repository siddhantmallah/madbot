Fair questions

Will it publish things to my site without asking?

Only if you turn the dial that far. At Watch it changes nothing; at Let it rip it publishes but never spends; at Full send it spends inside your budget. Everything it does is reversible in one click.

How is this different from an SEO tool?

An SEO tool hands you a list of problems and a monthly bill. MADBOT does the work — ships the pages, fixes the debt, earns the links, finds the buyers — and then tells you what changed.

What does it need from me to get started?

A website address. No tags, no keyword research, no onboarding call. It reads the site, tells you what it thinks you sell, and you correct it if it's wrong — corrections teach it faster than forms do.

How do I know it won't make things up about my product?

You write guardrails in plain English — “never say we're SOC 2 certified”. Anything public-facing, anything with a price tag, and anything it's under 60% sure about goes to an approvals queue instead of going out.

Can it get me cited by AI answer engines?

That's an explicit target, not a side effect. It tracks your citation share across nine answer engines every week and writes the quotable, schema-marked pages those engines prefer to cite.

What happens if I turn it off?

Everything it made is yours and stays on your site. Export the audit trail on the way out, and roll back anything you'd rather undo.
